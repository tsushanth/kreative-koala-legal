Delete Your Account and Data

KreativeKoalaSolutions LLC apps · Last Updated: November 11, 2026

You can delete your account, and the data we hold for you, from inside most of our apps. If you cannot use the app, or the app has no delete button, email us and we will do it for you. This page covers every KreativeKoalaSolutions LLC app that creates an account or stores data on our servers. Find your app below.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to puzzleverseai@gmail.com with:

  • The app name (for example "Pixora" or "Audexa").
  • The email address of your account, sent from that same address so we can confirm it is yours. If the app has no accounts, tell us which app and, if the app shows one, your device or install ID.
  • What you want deleted: your whole account and all data, or only certain data.

We will reply to confirm, and we will complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming your request (usually much sooner).

Apps with accounts

In-app menu names can differ slightly between versions. If you cannot find the button, use the email request above.

Pixora

  • In the app: Settings, Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm.
  • Deleted: your photos, videos, previews (including archived copies), albums and account record, and your sign-in record.
  • Details: pixora-web.fly.dev/delete-account. If you can still sign in after deleting, email us and we will remove the sign-in record by hand.
  • Not touched: photos that exist only on your phone.

Audexa

  • In the app: Profile, Delete Account.
  • Deleted in the app: your Audexa account record, the linked Gmail and Calendar connection, and your saved episodes.
  • Email us to remove the rest: your topic requests, "Ask the hosts" questions, any stored briefing audio, and your sign-in record. You can also remove Audexa's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

RiddleVerse and RiddleRush

  • RiddleVerse: follow the steps at puzzleverseai.com/delete-account.
  • RiddleRush: in the app, open the overflow menu and choose Delete Account. This removes only your sign-in. It does not delete the data held on our server: your scores, leaderboard entries and created puzzles are deleted on request. To have them deleted, use the email request above and include the email address of your Google account.

Elevate Careers

  • Accounts: Elevate Careers does not require an account; guest mode has no account to delete. An account is created only if you sign in with email and password, Google or LinkedIn.
  • In the app: Delete Account. Currently this removes only your Firebase sign-in. It does not delete data held on our servers.
  • By email: to delete the data we hold on our servers for your account, use the email request above and include the email address you signed in with.

TidyMail

  • In the app: Profile, Delete account, then confirm. On the web: sign in at tidymail.dev, then You, Privacy and data, Delete account. Details: tidymail.dev/delete-account.
  • Deleted, in this order: any subscription we bill through Stripe is cancelled first (if that fails, nothing is deleted and you can try again); TidyMail's access to your Google account (mail and calendar) is revoked and Gmail notifications are stopped; the stored copy of that access for every mailbox you connected is deleted; your stored audio and your TidyMail data are deleted (connected accounts, categories and reasons, to-dos, reply drafts, scheduled or pending messages, snoozes, assistant approvals, rules and action log, push-notification registrations, feedback, usage counts and, if you used them on the web, calendar connections and read receipts); and last, your sign-in record, which holds your email address. The sign-in record is removed only after everything else succeeded, so if something fails you can simply try again.
  • Not touched: your mail, which stays in your mailbox with Google or Microsoft; a Microsoft access permission (we delete our copy but cannot revoke it for you, so remove it in the security settings of your Microsoft account); payment and purchase records kept by Stripe and Google Play; anonymous usage statistics, which carry a random identifier and no account and expire after 180 days; and copies in routine backups and short-lived server logs until they expire.
  • Subscriptions: a Pro subscription bought through Google Play is not ended by deleting your account. Cancel it in Google Play, Menu, Subscriptions.
  • Disconnect only: you can remove TidyMail's access to Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • On your phone: the app's local data (settings, recent searches and the phone-only queues for snoozes, scheduled sends and reminders) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • If you cannot sign in: use the email request above from the address you used with TidyMail.

ScribeAI

  • In the app: sign in, open the menu on the home screen and choose Delete Account. The app shows how much you will lose; tap Delete Anyway (or Keep My Account to stop). Pick a reason from the list, which the button needs before it turns on, then tap Delete My Account. When our server confirms, the app tells you your account has been deleted and signs you out. If the server could not finish, the app shows an error and keeps you signed in; trying again is safe and continues where it stopped. If it keeps failing, use the email request above.
  • Deleted: your account and sign-in, your notes and everything generated from them (summaries, quizzes, flashcards, mind maps, diagrams), your recordings and their audio files, meeting records and meeting recordings, uploaded PDFs and scanned documents, generated audio (podcasts and read-aloud audio) and infographics, share links and the notes behind them, your subscription record on our server, usage records and the usage events the app sent us, and any connected-service and promo-code records tied to your account. Files are deleted first, then database records, then the sign-in; the account is reported as deleted only after the sign-in is gone.
  • Deleting a single note is different: it removes the note, its generated content and its recording record, but the original uploaded audio, document or meeting file can stay in storage until you delete your account. If you want those files gone, delete the account or email us and we will remove them.
  • Not deleted by us: your Google Play purchase and subscription records (held by Google); usage and advertising-measurement data the app already sent to Google (Firebase Analytics), Meta and TikTok, which they hold under their own policies and which we have no way to remove from the app; copies held by our service providers, namely meeting recordings kept by Recall.ai, and anything Deepgram or Anthropic keep under their own terms (we do not send them a deletion request); crash and failure reports, which are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 30 days; purchase-conversion events sent to our paywall service under a random ID; and routine backups and server logs, which expire on their own schedules. We keep a record of your deletion request (your email address and the date) if you ask by email.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, Menu, Subscriptions.
  • If you cannot sign in: email the address above from the email address of your account, with "ScribeAI" and what you want deleted. We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

MeetingMind

  • In the app: Settings, Delete account, then confirm. The same account is used in the MeetingMind web app, so deleting it in the app removes it there too. Details: meetingmind.org/delete-account.
  • Deleted, in this order: your audio, video and export files; the meeting media Recall.ai holds for your meeting bot recordings; any phone call recording still held at Twilio; your database records (recordings, transcripts, summaries, questions and answers, meetings, bot runs, phone numbers, share links, folders, calendar connections, integration settings and profile); and last, your sign-in account. If removing something at one of those providers fails, we retry it, or the app asks you to try again.
  • Not touched: data held under their own terms by Deepgram, Soniox, Anthropic, Resend, Firebase Analytics, Meta and TikTok; emails already sent; content already sent to Slack, Notion or a webhook you set up. Deletion does not revoke calendar access you granted in your Google or Microsoft account (remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in your Microsoft account), and it does not cancel a Google Play subscription.
  • One recording only: open the recording in the app and delete it. This removes its audio file, transcript, summary and questions from our storage and database. A copy that Recall.ai holds of a bot recording is removed when you delete your account.
  • If you cannot open the app: use the email request above from the address you signed up with.

VibeBuild

  • In the app: open the Account tab, tap Delete account, and confirm. If the app says to sign in again, sign out, sign back in, and repeat.
  • On the website: sign in at vibebuild.cc, open Settings, choose Delete account, and confirm. Details: vibebuild.cc/delete-account.
  • Deleted: your account record (name, email, profile picture link, notification token), your apps and all their versions, your chats with the builder, your coin balance and history, the records of your deployments, our usage events tied to your account, and your sign-in. Your published and preview sites are taken offline.
  • Email us to remove the rest: the copy of your app code kept in our private code repository, the preview screenshot images, and records of your subscription and usage limits. Email the address below from the address you signed up with.
  • Not touched: purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, and data already received by Google, Meta, TikTok and the AI providers, which follow their own policies. You can reset your Android advertising ID in Settings, under Google, then Ads.

SimplyApply

  • In the app: Account, Delete account (opens simplyappl.ai/delete-account).

ReadAloud AI

  • By email: there is no delete-account button in the app yet. Use the email request above with the subject "ReadAloud AI account deletion", from the email address of the Google account or email account you signed in with. We confirm by reply and handle these requests by hand, so it is not instant.
  • In the app, without deleting your account: delete a cloned voice in Settings, Voice Cloning. This removes the reference recording, the voice data made from it and the cached audio made with it. Usage statistics can be turned off in Settings. Voices you shared in the marketplace can be unshared there.
  • Deleted when we process your request: your account, and the data tied to it, including your cloned voices and their reference recordings, from the systems we run (Supabase and our voice-cloning service).
  • Kept after deletion: the record that you confirmed a voice was your own, including its consent recording, transcript and similarity score where we collected one, for 12 months after the voice is deleted; and a fingerprint (not the audio) of each file made with a cloned voice, for 90 days. Crash and error reports are not linked to your account and stay with our reporting service.
  • Not touched: purchase and subscription records held by Google Play and RevenueCat; data held by other providers under their own policies; and text read aloud through our speech service, whose retention we have not verified. Text and audio kept only on your phone are removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

SmartSpace (Cloud Archive)

  • Accounts: SmartSpace itself needs no account. Only the optional Cloud Archive creates an account, when you sign in with Google to archive files.
  • In the app: delete archived files one by one in the Archive screen. Tapping Retrieve also removes the file from the archive.
  • By email: to delete the Cloud Archive account and anything still stored for it, use the email request above.

Apps without accounts

These apps have no sign-in. Most keep everything on your phone, and uninstalling them removes it. Where an app stores something on our servers under a random ID instead of your name or email, it is listed here.

  • InboxIQ, TaskMasterBrowser, CalcMaster Pro: nothing is stored on our servers. Uninstall the app, or use the delete or clear buttons inside it, to remove your data.
  • GymLog: your workouts stay on your phone. If you turned on the optional anonymous usage counts, turning the switch off in Settings, Privacy deletes the random ID; counts already received are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Lullaby Haven: your favorites and saved narration stay on your phone. Narration requests carry the text of the story being narrated and a random install ID; email us if you want requests for your install removed.
  • VoxKey: if you chose to share dictated text, open Settings in the app and tap "Delete my shared data". For the Chrome extension, see VoxKey for Chrome: delete my data.
  • ClearVoice Recorder: recordings stay on your phone unless you turned on "Contribute Voice Data". Contributed recordings are linked only to a random install ID, not your name. To ask us to delete them, email us; we will make reasonable efforts to find them, and we may not be able to if the ID is no longer available.
  • SurfSense: email us with your device name and the approximate dates you used it. Unlinking a device in the app removes only its pairing.
  • NotifyPal: swipe a watcher to the left in the app and confirm; this stops its checks and asks our server to delete the watcher and its saved readings (if your phone is offline the request can fail, so check the list again when you are back online). Notification history is stored on your phone; clear it in NotifyPal Settings. If Android backup was on, an older copy may remain in your Google backup.
  • SecureVox: SecureVox has no sign-in and stores nothing about you on our servers, so there is no account to delete. Your recordings and transcripts are on your phone. To delete one, tap the bin icon next to it in the list (or open it and use the delete button); this permanently removes its audio and transcript and cannot be undone. SecureVox has no delete-all button. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings. If Android backup was on, an older copy may remain in your Google backup, which you manage in your Google account settings. If you turned on the optional anonymous crash reports, turn them off in SecureVox Settings; the reports are not linked to you, are deleted automatically after 30 days, and we cannot look up the ones from your phone. If you emailed us feedback or a problem report, email us and we will delete that message.

What happens to your data

  • What we delete: the account record and the content and data the app stored for you on our servers and with the service providers that store it for us, as listed in each app's section of the Privacy Policy.
  • Timeline: in-app deletion starts immediately. For email requests, we confirm your request and complete deletion within 30 days.
  • Backups and logs: deleted data can remain for a short time in routine backups and server logs, which expire on their own schedules.
  • What we may keep: a record of your deletion request (your email address and the date), and anything we are required by law to keep. Purchase and subscription records are held by Google Play under Google's policies.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, Menu, Subscriptions.
  • Local data: data stored only on your phone is not on our servers. Delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
