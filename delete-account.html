Delete Your Account and Data

KreativeKoalaSolutions LLC apps · Last Updated: November 10, 2026

You can delete your account, and the data we hold for you, from inside most of our apps. If you cannot use the app, or the app has no delete button, email us and we will do it for you. This page covers every KreativeKoalaSolutions LLC app that creates an account or stores data on our servers. Find your app below.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to puzzleverseai@gmail.com with:

  • The app name (for example "Pixora" or "Audexa").
  • The email address of your account, sent from that same address so we can confirm it is yours. If the app has no accounts, tell us which app and, if the app shows one, your device or install ID.
  • What you want deleted: your whole account and all data, or only certain data.

We will reply to confirm, and we will complete the deletion within 30 days of confirming your request (usually much sooner).

Apps with accounts

In-app menu names can differ slightly between versions. If you cannot find the button, use the email request above.

Pixora

  • In the app: Settings, Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm.
  • Deleted: your photos, videos, previews (including archived copies), albums and account record, and your sign-in record.
  • Details: pixora-web.fly.dev/delete-account. If you can still sign in after deleting, email us and we will remove the sign-in record by hand.
  • Not touched: photos that exist only on your phone.

Audexa

  • In the app: Profile, Delete Account.
  • Deleted in the app: your Audexa account record, the linked Gmail and Calendar connection, and your saved episodes.
  • Email us to remove the rest: your topic requests, "Ask the hosts" questions, any stored briefing audio, and your sign-in record. You can also remove Audexa's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

RiddleVerse and RiddleRush

  • RiddleVerse: follow the steps at puzzleverseai.com/delete-account.
  • RiddleRush: in the app, open the overflow menu and choose Delete Account. This removes your sign-in. To also delete your scores and other data held on our server, use the page above or email us.

TidyMail

  • In the app: Profile, Delete account.
  • Disconnect Gmail access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ScribeAI

  • In the app: open the menu on the home screen and choose Delete account.

MeetingMind

  • In the app: Settings, Delete account.

VibeBuild

  • In the app: Account, Delete account.

SimplyApply

  • In the app: Account, Delete account (opens simplyappl.ai/delete-account).

ReadAloud AI

  • By email: use the email request above and include the email address you signed in with.

SmartSpace (Cloud Archive)

  • Accounts: SmartSpace itself needs no account. Only the optional Cloud Archive creates an account, when you sign in with Google to archive files.
  • In the app: delete archived files one by one in the Archive screen. Tapping Retrieve also removes the file from the archive.
  • By email: to delete the Cloud Archive account and anything still stored for it, use the email request above.

Apps without accounts

These apps have no sign-in. Most keep everything on your phone, and uninstalling them removes it. Where an app stores something on our servers under a random ID instead of your name or email, it is listed here.

  • InboxIQ, TaskMasterBrowser, CalcMaster Pro: nothing is stored on our servers. Uninstall the app, or use the delete or clear buttons inside it, to remove your data.
  • GymLog: your workouts stay on your phone. If you turned on the optional anonymous usage counts, turning the switch off in Settings, Privacy deletes the random ID; counts already received are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Lullaby Haven: your favorites and saved narration stay on your phone. Narration requests carry only a random install ID; email us if you want requests for your install removed.
  • VoxKey: if you chose to share dictated text, open Settings in the app and tap "Delete my shared data". For the Chrome extension, see VoxKey for Chrome: delete my data.
  • ClearVoice Recorder: recordings stay on your phone unless you turned on "Contribute Voice Data". Contributed recordings are linked only to a random install ID, not your name. To ask us to delete them, email us; we will make reasonable efforts to find them, and we may not be able to if the ID is no longer available.
  • SurfSense: email us with your device name and the approximate dates you used it. Unlinking a device in the app removes only its pairing.
  • NotifyPal: swipe a watcher to the left in the app and confirm; this stops its checks and deletes its saved readings from our server. Notification history is stored only on your phone; clear it in NotifyPal Settings.

What happens to your data

  • What we delete: the account record and the content and data the app stored for you on our servers and with the service providers that store it for us, as listed in each app's section of the Privacy Policy.
  • Timeline: in-app deletion starts immediately. For email requests, we confirm your request and complete deletion within 30 days.
  • Backups and logs: deleted data can remain for a short time in routine backups and server logs, which expire on their own schedules.
  • What we may keep: a record of your deletion request (your email address and the date), and anything we are required by law to keep. Purchase and subscription records are held by Google Play under Google's policies.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, Menu, Subscriptions.
  • Local data: data stored only on your phone is not on our servers. Delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
