Delete my VoxKey for Chrome data

Only text samples you chose to share are stored by us (never audio). They are identified by a random install ID, not your name or Google account.

Easiest: from the extension

Open the extension's Settings, then press Delete my shared data. It deletes everything stored under your install ID and turns sharing off.

If you already uninstalled

Email puzzleverseai@gmail.com with your install ID (shown in the extension's Settings, under the sharing section; you can copy it there) and we will delete everything stored under it and reply to confirm.

If you cleared the extension's storage or uninstalled without saving the ID, we cannot tell which samples are yours. They are deleted automatically after 180 days.

Other data

Audio is not stored by us. Per-invite usage counters (seconds used) are kept to enforce the free allowance and contain no dictated text. See the privacy policy.
