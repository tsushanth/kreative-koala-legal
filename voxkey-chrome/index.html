VoxKey for Chrome (invite beta)

VoxKey for Chrome lets you dictate into the text box you are using. Press a shortcut, speak, press it again, and the cleaned-up text is typed at your cursor. It is an invite-only beta from KreativeKoalaSolutions LLC and is free for now; the free period may end.

Before you install: what happens to your voice

  • While you dictate, your audio is sent live to our server, which passes it to speech-recognition and text-cleanup services we use (Soniox for speech-to-text; Fireworks AI and Google Gemini for text cleanup; Cloudflare hosts our server). We do not store the audio. Those services process it under their own terms.
  • We do not read the page you are on, its URL, or the text already in the box. VoxKey only types the result at your cursor when you ask it to.
  • The microphone is on only between starting and stopping a dictation. Chrome shows its recording indicator while it is on.
  • Optional and off by default: you can choose to share the text of your dictations and your corrections to help improve VoxKey. Only the person who runs VoxKey reads samples. They are deleted after 180 days. Never audio, never page content.
  • Not available in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.

The extension asks you to read this and agree, in the extension itself, before anything is sent.

Install

Invite beta only: you need an invite code from us. (The Chrome Web Store listing link will be added here once the extension is approved.)

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain terms: we use data only to provide and improve VoxKey dictation, never for advertising, credit decisions or sale, and we do not transfer it except to the vendors that process it for us or as the law requires. Details in the privacy policy.
